About us

Maria Pyramids House is a hotel in Giza. We keep things simple: a comfortable room, a friendly welcome at the front desk, and a team that can help you plan your days in Egypt.

We serve meals at the hotel and arrange tours to the Pyramids and other places nearby, so you can sort out your whole stay in one place.

To book or ask a question, choose your dates below and send us a message on WhatsApp. We reply with availability and the rate for your stay.
