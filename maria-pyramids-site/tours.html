Tour10

Bahariya Oasis & White Desert

Two days and one night in the Western Desert, with a camp under the stars between the Black and White Deserts.

  • 2 days
  • 1 night in a desert camp
Itinerary
Day 1Cairo to Bahariya Oasis, then the Black and White Desert
Your guide collects you from your hotel in Cairo and drives you to Bahariya Oasis, about 360 km southwest of Cairo and roughly four hours by road. After lunch you visit the Black Desert and the natural hot spring of El Haize (St. George Oasis), then the quartz crystals of Crystal Mountain and the rock formations of the Valley of Agabat. Meals: lunch and dinner.
Day 2Bahariya Oasis and the drive back to Cairo
After breakfast at the campsite you drive to Bahariya Oasis to see the Golden Mummies, two nobles' tombs from the 26th dynasty at this large burial site, and the Tomb of Bannantiu. You then climb the Black Mountain and have lunch in Bahariya before driving back to Cairo. Meals: breakfast and lunch.
Included
  • Hotel pick-up and return
  • Entrance fees to the historical sites mentioned
  • Private air-conditioned vehicle
  • Private 4x4 into the White and Black Deserts
  • One night in a desert camp
  • 1 breakfast, 2 lunches and 1 dinner
  • Bottled water during the trip
  • Desert expert
  • All taxes and service charges
